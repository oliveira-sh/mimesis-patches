# BetterMimics changelog

## 1.0.0
- First version (host only): one voice per mimic for the whole level (the game mixed everyone's), the same person as the persona it copies, quiet while that person is near it, no talking over people, reply-like answers after a human pause (radio lines and the lines for being hit or attacking stay the game's own); standing mimics look at whoever talks or stares at them, glance away and look around (up close the game's trained movement model keeps the looking); wandering mimics pick up scrap they spot, also on their way (never from the tram; dropped if killed or when they take up a weapon) and put it down later; they join players' dances and emote now and then; they walk instead of flip-flopping into a run every few seconds. What a mimic is doing stops at once when it's hurt, and nothing runs while it's stunned. Each part can be turned off in the Patches window.
