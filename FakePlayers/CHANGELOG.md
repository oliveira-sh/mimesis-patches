# FakePlayers changelog

## 1.0.0
- First version: while hosting, F7 opens a panel that adds the game's own dummy players to your level and sets them: kill or revive, health, radiation, bring them over, have them follow you, make them talk, remove them. The same for yourself (kill or revive yourself, your radiation and health). Off by default; only the host needs it.
- With Hallucinations' Debug keys on, fake players can be sent hallucinations: the host's game plays their side.
- Fake players added after a level started stay alive: the game kills a player who joins a level late (3 s after), and fake players are exempt. Real players are untouched.
